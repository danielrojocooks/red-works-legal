Privacy Policy

Dock Check-In, by Red Works LLC
Effective September 21, 2026

What the app stores

Dock Check-In records dock purchases from commercial fishermen: fisherman names and license numbers, vessel information, species, weights, prices, and dates. When it is connected to QuickBooks Online, it reads vendors, products, invoices, and estimates, and it creates bills.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored on a single computer at the business, on its private network. There are no cloud servers and no copies held by Red Works LLC.

Who sees it

Only the business's own staff, through the app on the shop's network.

Who it is shared with

Landing reports required by law (species, weight, price, harvester license, and dealer license) are sent to ACCSP through SAFIS. QuickBooks data is never sent to ACCSP or to anyone else. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

QuickBooks access

The app connects through Intuit's official sign-in and asks only for accounting access. The business can disconnect at any time, which revokes access at Intuit.

Security

Connection credentials are stored only on the business's computer and are never included in the app's code or database. All traffic to Intuit and ACCSP is encrypted.

Retention

Records are kept for as long as the business needs them, including for regulatory recordkeeping, and are deleted at the business's request.
